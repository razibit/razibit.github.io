What to evaluate

Open a concept to see it larger. Compare the first impression, project readability, content density, and personal feel. Mobile views show the introduction through the first project; the rest of each page continues below.

Shared design commitments

  • Single-page section links; no portfolio content hidden in tabs. The controls above switch review images only.
  • Normal body type around 16px, supporting type around 14px, restrained headings, and natural letter spacing. Intended typefaces: Inter, Source Sans 3, and IBM Plex Sans respectively; raster mockups are approximate.
  • No decorative animation, parallax, cursor effects, or moving cards. Final links will retain underlines and controls a visible 3px focus outline.
  • A compact footer, inspired by Una’s personal sign-off, with a clearly labeled signature placeholder.

Tablet behavior for the selected direction

At roughly 834px wide, Profile & Practice moves its rail into a normal-flow introduction and wrapping navigation. Personal Editorial retains its centered reading flow. Work in Focus removes the narrow section-label margin and stacks project media above text when two columns become cramped. Exact breakpoints will be tested in the chosen implementation.
